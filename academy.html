Welcome to Narek Ohanyan’s Online Academy

Pioneering accessible climate education in Armenia.

Here you will learn to read the planet the way a scientist does and act on it the way an organiser does — from the feedback loops that drive a warming climate, to the policy levers that bend it, to the small daily practice that makes any of it stick. Every lesson is filmed, subtitled and signed, because knowledge that only reaches some people is not really open at all.

  • Sign language throughout
  • Armenian & English subtitles

A note on accounts

Your account is a real one. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, an established authentication service: your password is hashed and stored there, and this website never sees it, never stores it, and could not read it if it wanted to.

  • Your progress is saved to a database against your account, so it follows you to another browser or another device — just sign in.
  • Only you can read it. Access is enforced by the database itself, so no other learner can reach your progress even by asking for it directly.
  • What is kept is deliberately small: your name, your email address, and which lessons and quizzes you have finished. Nothing else.

The course is complete and free, and your certificate is generated on your own device. If you would like your account and progress deleted, ask and it will be removed.
