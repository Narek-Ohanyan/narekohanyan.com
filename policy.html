02

Meeting with the President of the UN General Assembly

Bilateral diplomatic engagement · 27 July 2022

Held a bilateral meeting with H.E. Abdulla Shahid, President of the 76th UN General Assembly, during his historic first visit to Armenia, alongside UN Resident Coordinator a.i. Mrs. Lila Pieters Yahia.

Our discussion centred on critical national environmental vulnerabilities — the accelerating desertification of the Ararat Valley, and the ecological and social impacts of Armenia's heavy economic reliance on the mining sector. I presented my ongoing environmental initiatives and advocated for enhanced United Nations support in transitioning Armenia away from resource extraction and toward a sustainable, dematerialized green economy.

As a member of GYBN Armenia I am dedicated to mobilizing youth to halt biodiversity loss and advocate for nature-based solutions. UNICEF Green Campaign: Zero Waste Living

UNICEF Armenia, Austrian Development Agency & Ministry of Environment · August 2021

In collaboration with UNICEF, the Austrian Development Agency and the Ministry of Environment, I joined a nationwide campaign advocating zero-waste living. Using digital advocacy, I translated complex environmental threats into accessible, everyday behavioural changes. Beyond the online campaign I established a local eco-club in the Ararat region to drive hands-on community projects — grounded in the belief that protecting nature is a fundamental act of defending our homeland.
