The book · 2026

The Overshoot

Life After the 1.5°C Limit

We have exited the Goldilocks Zone.

For thirty years the organising principle of climate policy was a red line: do not cross 1.5°C. The Overshoot begins with a single scientific reality — we are going to cross it — and maps the terrain on the other side.
